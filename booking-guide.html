Booking

When you really need to book

Plan ahead and know what you want before the booking window opens — for both weeks and points.

Plan ahead

Everyone's different, but let's be honest — everyone wants the most desired days at the most desired resort. The best way to get that is to book as soon as the booking window opens for those dates. Use the "When can I book?" link on the owners' site to find out exactly when your booking window opens — that'll be at least a year in advance, so be ready.

If you don't get your first choice

If you try to book early but don't get your desired days, you still have options. You can book other dates so you have something close, keep trying on a regular basis, or get on the waitlist. If you keep trying, that literally means daily, weekly, even hourly — whatever you can handle personally. Inventory can change for several reasons; one family may have made the same reservation and then decided it wasn't going to work out and canceled, as an example.

For the waitlist, you'll need to contact Owner Services to request it. Just know that you must give up the points required for that stay at the time you submit the waitlist request. So if something else comes up that you want instead, those points may be tied up — you'll need to call back in to free them up for booking something different.

Know your points and your tier

Keep track of your points so you know how many are available — the owner site does this for you as well. Review the Owner Benefit Levels to know what your owner status provides you for booking options. Here's an example table:

The more points you own, the earlier you can book — especially for a short-stay trip. For "Owner"-tier bookings of 7+ nights at 13 months, you'll pay a point premium: 10% on top of the actual reservation cost.

Check the cost before you book

Use the points calculator to figure out how many points your desired stay will actually cost, so you know whether you have enough. Consider banking and borrowing as you plan your trips out. The owners' site has a points calculator built in — we've also included one on the points page of this site.

The term "timeshare" originated in Europe, where multiple families would buy a vacation cottage together and split the time evenly throughout the year. That's close to what a week owner at MVC has — you split time at a resort and share it with other owners. When you purchased, you were designated a time, a unit size, and a view.

Simpler than points, but still worth planning

Booking a week has a lot fewer requirements than booking points. You don't need a calculator, and you don't need to decide which villa size works best — but you do need to know your booking window, especially for desirable weeks. The time you own is either fixed or floating.

Fixed weeks

Fixed weeks at a resort are literally fixed by the calendar — week 26 every year, for example. There aren't many of these left in MVC, but booking is simple: you just go in and book your only option.

Floating weeks

Floating weeks make up the majority of MVC weeks. That means you own within a season and aren't guaranteed the same week every year. The booking tool includes a calendar that shows you your season and the check-in days of the week available to you. Not all days are available for check-in — most are Friday, Saturday, or Sunday, though some resorts offer other days too. Each season has more desirable weeks than others, so if you want a specific week, plan for it.

Platinum Plus

Platinum Plus is a season that includes very desirable weeks at a given resort. The catch is that those weeks are usually not consecutive within the season — it may include the July 4th week, Christmas week, and Easter week, as an example. You're guaranteed one of those weeks, but it's likely you'll want one more than the others. Again: plan ahead and know when you can book.

Other booking options

Depending on the resort, you may have other options to book, like a split week or lockoff options. Check out the glossary for those terms.

If your date is already taken

If your desired check-in date is already taken, you can either keep checking in case someone cancels, or book another week instead. You can also decide whether you want to deposit into Interval and try to exchange into your desired week.
