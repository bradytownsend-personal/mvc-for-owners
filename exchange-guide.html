Exchange

Trading through Interval

How trading power is calculated, and the mistake that quietly costs owners their best trades.

Each use year, as an owner, you need to decide what you want to do with your ownership. In this guide, we'll talk about the exchange option.

What is an exchange?

An exchange is when you give up your vacation time to the exchange company, Interval International, and get a different vacation in return. For week owners, that means giving up the week reservation. For points owners, it means giving up a certain number of points, based on what you want to get back.

Trading power

Interval uses its own proprietary algorithm to determine trading power. The higher your trading power, the better chance you have of getting what you request. Interval uses the following to determine your deposit's trading power:

  • Travel Demand Index: a score derived from the timeframe and location
  • Resort Quality: MVC resorts should rate Elite or Premier, the highest quality tiers
  • Unit Size/Capacity: a 2-BR or 3-BR has higher trading power than a studio
  • Deposit Timing: the earlier you deposit your unit, the more power you have
  • MVC Priority: an MVC-to-MVC exchange gets priority over a non-MVC-to-MVC exchange

How to get more out of Interval

MVC resorts sit at the top of the quality ladder at Interval. To incentivize MVC owners, depositing a week will often get you one exchange week plus an Accommodation Certificate (AC) in return. The AC can get you another week for a relatively small fee.

Another option: if you own a lock-off unit, you could lock it off into two units, then deposit both into Interval — or use one and exchange the other. Lots of options.

Don't wait to deposit. Your odds of getting what you want are much better if you deposit early and request what you want right away. Traveling with another MVC owner and hoping you can both exchange into a specific resort? Call Interval and ask them to link your exchange requests, so they only confirm when both can be confirmed together.

Deposit First vs. Request First

When depositing a week for exchange, you have two options:

  • Deposit First: You give up your unit right away and request availability at another resort or set of resorts. Once you do this, Interval has full rights to your reservation to give to someone else.
  • Request First: You have a week you'd want to deposit into Interval in exchange for something else, but you wait to make sure you get what you want before giving up your own reservation.
