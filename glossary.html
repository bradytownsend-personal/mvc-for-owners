MVC Glossary

Every ownership term used across this site, in one place — alphabetized for quick lookup instead of scattered across each guide. If I'm missing any, let me know.

# A B C E F H I L M O P R S T U V W
  • 12-month Booking For Weeks: Any week owned can be booked 12-months in advance of check-in. The important part to know is that may not be exactly 365 days ahead. The earliest day to book is determined by the first available check-in day for the week you want. If a resort has check-ins on Fri, Sat, Sun, and you want to check-in on a Saturday, the 12-month booking date will be 12 months before the Friday. It is best to use the 'When Can I Reserve' link on the MVC site, under Use Weeks.
    For Points: For Member and Select owners, book 7 nights or more, 365 days ahead. For Executive and above, book one night or more.
  • 13-month Booking For Weeks: This early booking option is only available to owners with multiple week deeds. The options are to book multiple weeks consecutively (2 weeks in a row) or concurrently (2+ units, same week) at the time of booking. This can be done with weeks at the same resort or different resorts. Not all weeks inventory is available at 13-weeks, to provide single week owners inventory to the same vacation time.
    For Points: Executive owners and above are able to book one night or more as early as 13-months in advance. Select owners can book 7 nights or more. If you are Owner status, you pay a 20% premium to make a reservation this early. Use the 'When Can I Reserve' link on the MVC site, under Use Points to find the exact date.
  • Abound This is the official name of the current MVC points program. If you own points or have weeks enrolled in points, you are part of the Abound program. The program allows you to use your points to book stays at Marriott Vacation Club, Sheraton Vacaction Club, Westin Vacation Club, Grand Residences and The Ritz-Carlton Club locations within the program.
  • Accommodation Certificate (AC) Members of Interval International will be encouraged to use Interval International for more stays by offering these certificates. An example is if you deposit a MVC resort to II for exchange, they may give you an AC on top of your deposited week. To use the AC, you will need to follow the guidelines provided, which usually has a timeframe and fee structure. This is a good way to take additional week trips. The easiest way to use/plan these is to go to your II account and find them under Exchange. You might be able to get a fantastic deal!
  • Banking A points owner uses this option to extend the usage period of unused points. The banking deadline is defined by your ownership level. The extension time period is also defined by your ownership level, at least 12 months. Once points are banked, that action cannot be reversed. Using the banking option could also allow an owner to book a trip costing more than their single year allocation.
  • Beneficial Interest (BI) When purchasing points, the number of points are broken down into chunks of points. Each chunk is 250 points. An owner with 1000 points, owns 4 BI's (250 x 4 = 1000). An owner does not need to know specifics of their BI's, other than the usage year. Not all BI's have a usage year of Jan 1 - Dec 31.
  • Benefit Levels Directly related to the ownership level. Each ownership level includes benefits that improve with the level. Items like banking, booking, week election, etc improve with higher levels. Go HERE for a full details.
  • Bonvoy Marriott International's official travel loyalty program. While not directly related to MVC ownership, you can increase your Bonvoy points as an owner, by accruing points during your stay. Having the Marriott credit card is also helpful. When using your ownership, Bonvoy benefits that you get at a hotel are not included for your stay, such as late checkout, free breakfast, etc. Points owners are automatically given at least Gold Bonvoy status while they own.
  • Borrowing A points owner who wishes to use more points than available within the current use year, has the option to borrow points from a future year. This strategy allows for more points this year, but obviously takes away from the future year. Once points are borrowed, that action cannot be reversed.
  • Club Dues A commonly forgotten fee associated with being part of the Abound program. The dues also cover the II account associated with your Abound points. This is similar to a Maintenance Fee. Per MVC, they use this fee to maintain the points program and the ability to exchange into any MVC resort. The fee is based on your ownership level, the more points you have access to, the more your fee. As of this writing, the fees are between $255 and $320 per year, due in December.
  • Club Points (aka Destination Club Points) The old name of Abound. See Abound, above.
  • Concurrent Weeks Only valid for weeks owners. When an owner of multiple weeks books more than 1 week for the same vacation time. This is a 13-month benefit to multi-week owners, providing them an advantage of booking early.
  • Consecutive Weeks Only valid for weeks owners. When an owner of multiple weeks books more than 1 week back to back (2+ weeks vacation time). This is a 13-month benefit to multi-week owners, providing them an advantage of booking early.
  • Elect Only valid for weeks owners that have their week(s) enrolled in the Abound program. Each year, the week owner has the ability to decide whether they want to use their week as a week to book at their home resort, or if they would like to use their week for points, instead. The act of selecting points is called Electing points.
  • Encore Package A marketing tool used by the sales team to get prospective buyers into a resort. It's a vacation stay at one of the resorts for anywhere from 3–7 nights, at a much lower cost than booking as a random guest. The catch: you have to attend a sales presentation during your stay, scheduled for you as part of the package. Skip the presentation, and you'll be charged full rack rate for the entire stay instead of the discounted rate. Used the right way, these can be a good way to get extra stays at resorts — just say no at the presentation.
  • Enroll Only valid for weeks owners. If a week owner is not already part of the Abound program, they have the ability to Enroll that week, or multiple weeks. This would then allow them to decide, yearly, whether to use their week(s) as a week or choose points in lieu of the week. When Abound was first announced, there was a fee to enroll, but it should be free today. Initiate this from the MVC owners site, or call Owner Services.
    NOTE: A resale week purchased after June 20, 2010 is not able to be enrolled, although MVC sales does offer enrollment with additional purchases, a lot. See a sales rep.
  • Exchange Using your ownership to get something different. Mostly a weeks "thing". For instance, giving up your week at resort A to book a week at resort B, through Interval International. Internal exchange is now done via the Abound points program. From the points side, there are additional exchange options MVC offers, like exchanging points for cruises, hotels, owners events and more. Those exchange rates are very poor, so just keep that in mind when reviewing your options.
  • Exit Program Owners that no longer wish to own within MVC have options. MVC offers the Exit Program. This is essentially where they expect you to hand over your ownership. Generally, you will pay MVC closing costs to give back weeks for $0. Expect similar with points give back. You should consider the resale options and price aggressively if you want out quickly.
  • Fixed Week A fixed week locks you into the same calendar week every year - maybe the same check-in day too. Not all resorts have fixed week deeds. For some resorts, there are fixed weeks for special times of year, such as spring break, Christmas, July 4, etc.
  • Floating Week A float week gives you a window (say, "any week in Gold season") within which you reserve your specific dates, usually on a first-come basis among owners in that float group/season. Check-in days vary by resort, where some may only offer Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and others offer additional days of the week to check-in.
  • Florida Club An internal exchange program offered at certain resorts in Florida. Not all resorts in Florida are part of this program, and not all units within the resorts are part of the program. The rules associated with booking using Florida Club differ from the normal weeks booking windows. If purchasing a resale week at a Florida resort, you should check if the specific deed identifies it as part of Florida Club if that is important to you.
  • Holding Account An Abound specific term. If you need to cancel a points reservation 60 days or less from the check-in date, the points do not go directly back into your account, they go into a holding account. The holding account limits your ability to re-use those points. The points can only be used to book a reservation a maximum of 60 days ahead of the check-in date.
  • Home Resort This is only relevant to Weeks-based owners. The deed purchased is specific to a single resort, which is the home resort.
  • Hybrid Owner An owner who owns both weeks and points. The week (or weeks) may or may not be enrolled in Abound. This is also a common sales offer — a "hybrid offer" — where the sales team tries to sell you a week at a specific resort along with additional points, so you end up owning both.
  • Interval International (II)

    A company, owned by Marriott Vacations Worldwide, used to perform exchanges. As a MVC owner, you may have an II account. Points owners automatically get a free II account as part of the Club Dues. Weeks enrolled will also be covered by the same account as points. Weeks owners, not enrolled, will need to pay for their II account.

    When depositing a week for exchange, you have two options:

    • Deposit First: You give up your unit right away and request availability at another resort or set of resorts. Once you do this, Interval has full rights to your reservation to give to someone else.
    • Request First: You have a week you'd want to deposit into Interval in exchange for something else, but you wait to make sure you get what you want before giving up your own reservation.
  • Inventory (Buckets)

    Have you wondered why you can’t make a reservation, but others can? This is sometimes because there are different types of inventory. There are 3 major buckets of inventory, and the buckets differ at every resort based on ownership. The buckets are weeks, points (Trust), and developer (MVC). The part that gets confusing is inventory can move to a different bucket. Here are some examples:

    • When a Weeks Owner reserves their week: Inventory stays in the Weeks Owner bucket. No movement occurs.
    • When a Weeks Owner deposits into Interval International: The week moves from Weeks Owner → Interval International (II) inventory. MVC loses access to that week.
    • When a Weeks Owner trades for Marriott Bonvoy Points: The week moves from Weeks Owner → Developer Inventory. MVC can:
      • Rent it for cash.
      • Move it into points (Trust) inventory.
      • Use it for Bonvoy points reservations.
    • When a Weeks Owner elects their week for Abound Points: This is the most misunderstood movement. The week moves from Weeks Owner → points (Trust) Inventory. The Trust now controls that week, and points owners can book it.
    • When MVC reacquires weeks (ROFR, foreclosure, buyback): Inventory moves from Weeks Owner → Developer Inventory. MVC may:
      • Sell it as a Trust interest (moving it into Trust permanently).
      • Sell it as a deeded week (usually in a bundle with points).
      • Use it for Bonvoy points reservations.
      • Rent it for cash.
    • When MVC sells a Trust interest: Inventory moves from Developer → Trust Inventory permanently.
  • Legacy Week A deeded ownership of a 7-night stay at a specific property. The term legacy is used because weeks ownership is considered the "old" way of purchasing MVC. The purchase of a resale week would also be considered a legacy week.
  • Lockoff A villa unit size, similar in size and amenities as a hotel room. Also known as a studio unit. The term lockoff can also be used for some 2-BR and 3-BR units that have the option to split the villa to a smaller size, enabling one owned unit to offer multiple usage options, such as 2 weeks stay or using part of your villa as a stay and part to deposit into II for an exchange.
  • Maintenance Fee (MF) The dreaded fee each owner pays to MVC to ensure the property is maintained to the high standards owners expect. The board of each resort is responsible for identifying the MF each year. For points owners, the MF is calculated using the MF of each resort and how many units of each resort are included within the overall points trust. Keep in mind the MF's will increase yearly, generally 3%, but varies year to year.
  • Owner Discount (at resort) A perk offered at many (maybe all) resorts, offering a discount for food and beverage purchases during your stay. Alcohol is normally not included. Some resorts require showing the card provided at check-in, others will provide the discount by simply asking for the Owner Discount.
  • Owner Discount (booking) When booking a cash stay at a MVC resort, owners can sometimes get a discount. The discount is based on your ownership level.
    2VC (35% off for Presidential/Chairman's Club)
    6VC (30% off for Executive)
    7VC (25% off for Select or un-affiliated Weeks owners)
    Based on availability. Don't expect this discount during peak time.
  • Ownership Levels Based on how many Abound points your ownership portfolio has in total, per year, you are within a specific Ownership Level. The level provides the details of the benefits you are provided. The levels are:
    Owner (less than 4000)
    Select (4000-6999)
    Executive (7000-9999)
    Presidential (10000-14999)
    Chairman's Club (15000 or more)
    Note: MVC is releasing new levels, above Chairman's Club soon.
  • Plus Points Sometimes offered as an incentive when purchasing directly from MVC. These are points that are placed within your account with specific usage guardrails. They are not able to be banked, borrowed, transferred or exchanged in any way. The points also have a usage deadline, usually 2 years or more. This allotment of points is one-time usage, will not be provided yearly.
  • Points Chart A table showing how many points a stay costs, based on resort, season, villa size, and even day of week. The same resort can cost very different amounts depending on the size of unit and time of year. To find the full listings, go to Use Points from the MVC owners site.
  • Rental A reservation booked by an owner, offered to someone else to use the reservation. Rentals are available via several websites and Facebook groups. When renting, due diligence is required to ensure you are not being scammed.
  • Reservation Window The timeframe when booking can occur for your stay. See 12-month booking and 13-month booking above for more detail.
  • ROFR (Right of First Refusal) A legal/contractual phrase used by MVC related to the resale of MVC ownership. Most, but not all, MVC ownership requires the seller to submit a ROFR document to MVC to determine if MVC would like to step in and become the buyer, instead of the intended buyer. As an example, if owner A wants to sell to buyer B for $4000, MVC has the right to allow the sale to go through or to step in and buy the same property for $4000 from the seller.
    Check rofr.net for some info on this topic. On that site, pass means the sale went through and fail means MVC stepped in to buy.
  • Season Resorts group weeks into demand tiers — often labeled something like Platinum, Gold, Silver, and Bronze. Higher-demand seasons (school holidays, peak weather) carry more trading power and hold resale value better. From a rental aspect, the higher the season, you can generally receive a higher rent value. Not all resorts use all of the season labels mentioned, some may have only 2 while others have up to 5. A Platinum-Plus season is for very high demand specific weeks.
  • Skim (enrolled weeks) A term owners coined when the points program was released. For week owners who enroll their week in the points program, each year they elect points for that week, the number of points they receive is less than the number of points it would actually cost to book that same week with points. Fictional example: you elect points for a week at Resort XYZ and receive 2,400 points. Booking that same time at XYZ with points might cost 2,500 points. That 100-point difference is the skim. That said, you'd rarely want to elect points just to use them at the same resort you already own — unless you were booking into a less expensive season than the one you're enrolled in.
  • Split Week At some resorts, weeks owners are offered the ability to use the split week option. This allows the owner to book a 3-night stay and a 4-night stay, seperately. The option provides 2 stays using one week of ownership, still for a total of 7-nights. If this option is available to you, it will show up when booking your week.
  • Trade See Exchange, above.
  • Transfer To take your ownership and put different owner names on the deed. There are specific legal requirements associated to this type of action, which we will not get into. The rules generally allow a simple transfer for direct relatives (son, daughter, parent). Others may require a resale.
  • Trust The bucket of points in the Abound program, whether owned by individuals or MVC. Points that come from enrolled weeks are not considered part of the trust, because the property behind that inventory is not fully conveyed to the trust. MVC sales may tell you that trust points have more power than enrolled points, but they have never provided proof that I have ever seen. A point is a point.
  • TUGBBS To my knowledge, the best place to become extremely knowledgeable about MVC. Hopefully this site too! Find TUGBBS HERE
  • Usage Year For Weeks: The yearly calendar is the usage year. Within the calendar, your season will more specifically identify when within the calendar you are able to book.
    For Points: The 12-month period that identifies the beginning and end of your usage period for each allotment of points. Not all points owner contracts are lined up with the calendar year, so this is a very important aspect of your ownership to understand. If you purchase additional points, to keep things easiest for you, purchase points with the same usage year as the rest of your ownership.
  • View Category Generally related to the location of the villa at the resort. For weeks, you get what you own. For points, you book what you want, the preferred views will cost more points to book. Some resorts may not have different view types, while others may have several. Each resort may use the view name differently, so if you really want to be able to see the ocean or the slopes, do some investigation to understand what the view type really means.
  • Villa Size Studio, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom, 3-bedroom — this should be self-explanatory. The biggest thing to consider is not all resorts provide the same amenities for the same villa size. For instance, a 2-bedroom at one resort may have two twin beds in bedroom 2, where others may have two queen beds. Full kitchen vs. limited kitchen/kitchenette is another difference to know about your week. The max occupants per villa is dictated by the villa size.
  • Waitlist A points booking option when the inventory you want is not available for you to book because it is "sold out". By calling Owner Services, you can request to be put on the waitlist for the resort, dates, unit size and view you want. The sooner you get on the waitlist, the better. If you do submit a waitlist request, know the points required for the stay will be pulled out of your account and held by MVC so the reservation can be booked immediately when it becomes available.
