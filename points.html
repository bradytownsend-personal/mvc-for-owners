Ownership types

Points-based ownership

Abound points provide flexibility — book any resort in the program, and choose your dates, villa size, and length of stay. Using points allows you to book throughout the year, assuming you have enough points for the reservation you want.

Curious what your points actually cover?

Enter your points balance and pick a resort to see which seasons, day types, and unit sizes fit — no chart math required.

What can I book?

With points ownership, you're allocated a set number of points each year rather than a specific week at a specific resort. You spend those points booking stays anywhere in the program — different resorts, different dates, different unit sizes, different trip lengths — as long as you have the points and the availability lines up.

That flexibility is the entire appeal, and it's also the entire trade-off. Weeks give you certainty; points give you options. Getting the most out of a points ownership means understanding how the system really works.

Enrolled week owners that elect points for the week have the same flexibility for that year, and any year the points are elected.

USE THEM HOWEVER YOU WISH

Flexibility vs. certainty, plus options

What you gain

More flexibility

Different resort every year if you want. Shorter or longer stays. A studio one year, a two-bedroom the next. Points adapt to whatever your travel plans look like this year, not what they looked like when you bought.

What you give up

Less certainty

No guaranteed unit at a guaranteed time. Popular resorts and peak seasons require booking early and can still sell out. If you always want the exact same week, that flexibility is a feature you'll never use.

Bank Points

Carry unused points into next year

Not going to use all your points this use year? Abound lets you bank them forward, giving you a bigger balance to work with the following year — useful for building toward a longer trip or a bigger unit. Banking has a deadline, so don't wait until your points are about to expire.

Borrow Points

Pull next year's points forward

Need more points than you currently have for a trip you want to book now? Borrowing lets you pull next year's allocation forward to use today — the trade-off is a smaller balance to work with next year, so it's worth planning ahead rather than missing out on a desired trip.

Rent Points From/To

Let someone else use them

If you need more points, but don't want to borrow, you can rent points from other owners. Once you find the right rental, it takes a 3-way call with MVC to get the points in your account. Same thing works the other way to rent your points to someone else.

Exchange via Interval

Trade for the wider network

Your points can also be deposited into Interval International, similar to a week, opening up resorts outside the MVC portfolio. Explore the Interval site to understand how many points you need to deposit to get the unit type and location you want. Based on availability.

Beyond just stays

Points open other doors too

Cruises, Tours & More

Points aren't just for resort stays

Beyond booking a week, points can typically be redeemed for cruises, guided tours, and other travel experiences through the program's partner offerings. The catch: exchange rates for these options tend to run worse than booking a resort stay directly, so treat it as a convenience for a special trip, not your default use of points.

Travel Insurance

Optional coverage from the unexpected

You have the option of buying travel insurance using your points instead of cash to protect against trip cancellations, interruptions, or other unexpected events. It's worth weighing against your own coverage (a credit card's travel benefits, an existing policy). Don't cancel! Get reimbursed for a missed stay for qualifying reasons.

Resort Credit

Extra spending money for your stay

Some resorts offer resort credit — a dollar amount you can use on-site for things like dining, spa services, or activities. Availability and amount vary by resort and promotion, so don't count on it being there; treat it as a nice-to-have rather than something to plan a trip around. Common conversion is 325 points for $100 credit.

Before you decide

Is points ownership right for you?

  1. Do your travel dates actually change year to year, or do you tend to want the same week every time?
  2. Are you interested in visiting multiple resorts across the portfolio, or mainly one or two?
  3. Does your party size change trip to trip — would you use the ability to book different villa sizes?
  4. Will you plan ahead and be ready to book as soon as booking windows open for the most desirable locations?
  5. Would you use banking or borrowing to build up points for a bigger trip every few years?

Wondering how this compares to a deeded week?

A week trades that flexibility for a locked-in resort, season, and unit — no chart, no booking window.
