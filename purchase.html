Getting started

What to think about before you buy

Weeks or points, direct or resale — the right answer depends on how you actually travel, not on what a presentation tells you that day.

First decision

Weeks or points?

Weeks

Predictable, resort-specific

A deeded interest in a specific resort, season, and villa size. Best if you know exactly where and when you want to go, year after year.

Points

Flexible, portfolio-wide

Second decision

Buying direct vs. buying resale

Same underlying ownership, often very different price — and often a different set of what comes with it.

Direct from MVC

Higher price, full benefits

Buying from the developer typically means full enrollment in the current points and exchange programs, financing incentives, and whatever perks are active at the time — but at full retail price.

Sales pitch info →

Resale

Lower price, fewer guarantees

The same deeded week or points can be bought resale for significantly less — but resale purchases aren't always eligible every perk. Does that matter to you? Maybe not.

Before you sign anything

Questions worth asking

  1. What's the maintenance fee history for this specific week or points package — has it been rising faster than typical?
  2. What are the transfer and closing costs, and who's paying them?
  3. Does the resort or HOA hold a right of first refusal that could slow down or block the sale?
  4. Are there special assessments pending or recently levied on this resort?
  5. If buying resale, is the seller using a licensed closing/transfer company, not just a handshake deal?
  6. What's the realistic resale value if you need to exit in a few years — not what the seller tells you, but what similar weeks have actually sold for?
